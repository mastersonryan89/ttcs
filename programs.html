Our Programs & Services

Our programs & services

Holistic services that meet people where they are

Six programs are running today. Two more launch in 2026.

Transition & Reentry Services

Supporting people returning to the community with guidance, resources and personalized planning for successful reintegration.

Workforce Development

Job readiness training, career coaching, résumé assistance, interview preparation and employment placement.

Second Chance Staffing

Connecting motivated individuals with employers who believe in second chances and long-term career success.

Housing Assistance

Help securing safe, stable housing plus the resources that support housing stability and independent living.

Life Skills Training

Financial literacy, communication, conflict resolution, goal setting, time management and healthy decision-making.

Peer-Led Support Groups

Safe, supportive spaces where people connect, share experiences, build resilience and encourage one another.

Launching 2026

Peer Support Services

One-to-one encouragement and mentorship from people with lived experience of recovery, reentry and personal transformation.

Launching 2026

Case Management

Individualized support to navigate community resources, set personal goals and coordinate services toward long-term success.

Our approach

Person-centered, not one-size-fits-all

Every person's journey is different. We provide individualized services that address the whole person — not just the most urgent need in front of them.

Through collaboration, accountability, compassion and community partnership, we help people build the skills, confidence and support systems that make independence last.
