Why choose us

Why Choose Us

One service is rarely enough.

A job without housing does not hold. Housing without recovery support does not hold. We address the whole picture at once, in one place, with one team.

The problem

People fall through the gaps between services

Someone leaving incarceration is often handed a list: one number for housing, another for treatment, another for employment, each with its own intake, its own waitlist and its own paperwork. Every handoff is a place to lose someone.

The need that shows up first is rarely the need that decides the outcome. A job falls apart because there is nowhere stable to sleep. Housing falls apart because recovery went unsupported. Progress in one area collapses when the others go unaddressed.

We were built to close those gaps rather than add another one.

Comprehensive support

Six areas, addressed together

Participants do not have to qualify for one thing at a time. These move in parallel, coordinated by the same team.

Employment

Job readiness training, career coaching, résumé and interview preparation, and placement through Second Chance Staffing.

Housing

Help securing safe, stable housing, plus the practical resources that keep a person housed once they are in the door.

Wellness

Connection to mental health care and follow-through afterward, so wellness is handled alongside everything else rather than after a crisis.

Recovery

Peer-led support groups where people in recovery connect, share honestly and build resilience with others who have been there.

Life skills

Financial literacy, communication, conflict resolution, goal setting, time management and healthy decision-making.

Community connection

Belonging is protective. We work to root people in relationships and community rather than leaving them independent but isolated.

What sets us apart

Why partners send people to us

One team, not a referral maze

Employment, housing and support services are coordinated internally. Your client explains their situation once.

In person from the first conversation

There is no online intake to disappear into. Participants meet a person at our High Street office and know who to come back to.

Lived experience in the room

Our peer-led groups are led by people who have been through recovery and reentry themselves, which changes what participants are willing to say out loud.

We stay past the first win

A placement is a milestone, not an exit. We stay engaged through the first months, when most arrangements are at their most fragile.

We support the employer too

Second Chance Staffing includes retention support on the employer side, so a hiring manager is not left managing a difficult stretch alone.

Small enough to answer the phone

We are a local organization with one office and posted hours. Calls reach a person who knows the participants by name.

For referral partners

What to expect when you send someone our way

For case managers, probation officers, discharge planners and community partners.

A straight answer on fit

If we are not the right resource for your client, we will say so on the call rather than starting a process that goes nowhere.

A person, not a queue

You will be talking to someone who can actually speak to capacity and timing, during posted business hours.

Warm handoffs, not cold referrals

We would rather coordinate directly with you than hand your client a phone number and hope it gets used.

Updates, with consent

With the participant's permission, we keep the referring partner informed about progress instead of going silent after intake.

For employers

A hiring pipeline with support attached

Second Chance Staffing connects you with pre-screened, motivated candidates, and it does not end at the offer letter. Hiring incentives and retention support come with the placement.

When something comes up in the first few months, you have someone to call who already knows the person and can help work it out.
