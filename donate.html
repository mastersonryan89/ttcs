Give now

Give once, or give every month

A one-time gift funds what is in front of us this week — bus passes, work boots, an application fee, a night that does not end on the street.

A monthly gift does something a one-time gift cannot. It lets us tell a participant we will still be here in March. Predictable income is what turns a good intention into a service somebody can count on.

Every donation is receipted. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Where it goes

What your gift actually pays for

We are a small organization. There is no marketing department here — money that comes in goes out to the things below.

Getting to work

Bus passes and gas cards. Transportation is the single most common reason a placement falls apart in the first month.

Showing up ready

Interview clothes, work boots, safety gear and tools. You cannot take the job if you cannot show up dressed for it.

Paperwork that unlocks everything

State ID, birth certificates, Social Security cards, licensing and application fees. Small costs that block everything behind them.

Keeping a roof

Housing search support and the deposits, application fees and first-week basics that stand between someone and a stable address.

The classes themselves

Space, materials and staff time for life skills training, job readiness and the peer-led support groups that run every week.

Staying in touch

The follow-up that happens after the job starts. It is the least visible part of the work and the part that decides whether it lasts.

The fine print

Tax deductibility and where you can check on us

Tiff Top Community Solutions is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive a written receipt for every gift.

We do not sell, rent or trade donor information. Ever. If you want your gift kept anonymous, say so when you give and it will be.

Questions about a donation, a receipt or a pledge? Call 614-502-0396 or email tiffany@ttc-solutions.org and you will get a person, not a ticket number.
