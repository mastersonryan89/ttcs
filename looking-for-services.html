Looking For Help?

You are in the right place.

Coming home from prison or jail is hard. So is getting sober. So is finding work and a place to live at the same time. You do not have to figure it out alone.

Call 614-502-0396 Come see us
Two ways to start

There is no form to fill out

You will talk to a real person. Nothing you say goes on a website.

Call us

Call 614-502-0396 Monday through Friday, 10am to 6pm. Tell us what is going on. If we cannot help with something, we will try to tell you who can.

Come in

Our office is at 1511 S. High St, Columbus, OH 43207. You can walk in during our hours. You do not need an appointment and you do not need to bring anything.

What it costs

Nothing. Our programs are free. We will never ask you for money to get help.

What we help with

Here is what we can do

Most people need help with more than one of these. That is normal, and it is fine to ask about all of them.

Coming home

If you are getting out soon or just got out, we help you make a plan. What to do first, what papers you need and where to go for each one.

Finding a job

We help you build a résumé, practice interviews and figure out how to talk about your record. Then we help you apply.

Getting hired

We work with employers who hire people with records. They know your background before you walk in, so you are not hiding anything.

A place to live

We help you look for safe, steady housing and connect you with the programs that can help you keep it.

Everyday skills

Money and budgeting, handling conflict, setting goals, managing your time. The things that make the rest of it stick.

People who get it

Support groups led by people who have been where you are. A place to talk honestly without being judged for it.

What to expect

What happens when you come in

Nobody is going to put you on the spot. This is a conversation, not a test.

First

We talk

You tell us what is going on and what you need most right now. You only share what you want to share.

Next

We make a plan

We figure out the first two or three steps together. Not twenty steps. Just enough to get moving.

Then

You get started

We sign you up for what fits — a class, a support group, job help, housing help — and tell you when and where to show up.

After

We stay with you

We check in. When something goes wrong, you call us and we work on it. You are not done with us after one visit.

Straight answers

Things people worry about before they call

If one of these is the reason you have not called yet, read it and then call anyway.

“My record is bad.”

That is who we work with. We are not going to be surprised by anything you tell us, and we are not here to judge it.

“I messed up before.”

If you tried something and it did not work, you can come back. People start over here all the time. That is the whole point.

“I do not have ID or papers.”

Come anyway. Getting your ID, birth certificate and Social Security card sorted out is often the first thing we help with.

“I have no way to get there.”

Call us and say so. Transportation is one of the biggest problems our people have, and it is something we work on with you.

“Will you tell my PO or my family?”

We keep what you tell us private. We do not share it without your permission, except in the rare cases the law requires. Ask us and we will explain.

“I am not sure I am ready.”

You can call and just ask questions. Nothing happens that you do not agree to. Asking is not signing up.

Right now

If you need help this minute

Our office is open Monday through Friday, 10am to 6pm. We are not a crisis line and we are not open at night or on weekends.

If you are in danger, thinking about hurting yourself, or someone needs medical help, use the numbers in the box. They answer 24 hours a day, every day.

Then call us when we open. We will still be here.

24-hour help

988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text 988 any time, for yourself or for someone else. It covers mental health and substance use.

911 — for a medical emergency or if someone is in immediate danger.

Both are free and both are open all night.

For family and friends

Trying to help someone else?

You can call us and ask questions for them. We will tell you what we do and what the first step looks like.

There is one thing we cannot do: sign someone up who has not decided to come in. That part has to be theirs. What you can do is tell them we are here, and that it is free.

Ask us about them when you come in.
